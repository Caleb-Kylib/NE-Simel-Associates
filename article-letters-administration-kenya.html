Letters of Administration in Kenya: What You Need to Know

6 min read March 5, 2026

Letters of Administration are the legal documents issued by a Kenyan court to authorize someone to administer the estate of a person who died without a valid will (intestate). Understanding this process is essential for families dealing with intestate succession.

1. When Are Letters of Administration Needed?

Letters of Administration are required when:

  • The deceased died without a will (intestate)
  • The will is invalid or cannot be found
  • The named executor in a will is unwilling or unable to act
  • There is no valid Grant of Probate

2. Who Can Apply for Letters of Administration?

Under Kenyan law, the following persons (in order of priority) can apply:

  • Surviving spouse(s): Widow or widower
  • Children: Of the deceased, including adopted children
  • Parents: Father or mother of the deceased
  • Siblings: Brothers and sisters of the deceased
  • Other relatives: In the order specified by the Law of Succession Act
  • Creditors: In some circumstances, with court permission

3. Application Process

The application involves these key steps:

  • Filing P&A 5 form at the High Court with supporting documents
  • Providing death certificate of the deceased
  • Submitting list of assets, liabilities, and beneficiaries
  • Providing consent from other beneficiaries
  • Publishing notice in Kenya Gazette and local newspaper
  • Waiting for 30-day objection period

4. Duties and Responsibilities of Administrators

Once appointed, administrators must:

  • Collect and preserve estate assets
  • Pay valid debts and funeral expenses
  • Prepare inventory of assets and liabilities
  • Distribute estate according to intestacy rules
  • Keep accurate accounts and file them with the court
  • Obtain court confirmation before distribution

5. Differences from Grant of Probate

Aspect Grant of Probate Letters of Administration
When Used When there's a valid will When there's no valid will (intestate)
Appointed Person Executor (named in will) Administrator (court-appointed)
Distribution Basis According to will terms According to Law of Succession Act
Application Forms P&A 1, P&A 4 P&A 5
Court Confirmation Not always required Always required before distribution
