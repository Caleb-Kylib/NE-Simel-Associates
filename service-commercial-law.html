Business Infrastructure

Enabling Corporate Growth & Upholding Regulatory Standards

Navigating the commercial landscapes in Kenya requires a solid legal blueprint. NE Simel Associates partners with local businesses, agricultural consortiums, startups, and foreign investors to construct and protect their corporate infrastructures.

Our commercial lawyers offer detailed corporate consultancy, starting from business formation, structuring joint ventures, and drafting executive contracts, up to auditing compliance under the Companies Act. We aim to identify and isolate legal risks proactively, ensuring that your commercial transactions are legally airtight and structured for continuous growth.

Key Focus Areas

Company Registration & Restructuring

Structuring public and private limited companies, limited liability partnerships (LLPs), non-profits, and executing corporate reorganization plans.

Joint Ventures & Shareholder Agreements

Drafting joint venture partnerships, share option pools, and shareholder covenants outlining funding terms, governance, and dispute resolutions.

Licensing & Regulatory Compliance

Guiding companies through sector-specific licensing, data protection compliance, and executing corporate governance audits.

Tax Law & Business Advisory

Structuring transactions tax-efficiently, managing corporate tax exposures, and offering advisory services on international contracts.
