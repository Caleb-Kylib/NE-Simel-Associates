Consultation preparation checklists

Use these lists to organize questions and documents before a consultation. Tick items on screen or print this page to save it as a PDF.

Before a land purchase consultation

Bring copies where available. Do not email original identity or title documents through an unsecured channel.

Before a succession or probate consultation

Only gather records you can lawfully access. The advocate can advise which documents are needed for your situation.

Important: These checklists are general preparation aids, not legal advice or a complete list of requirements. Requirements depend on the facts and current law. Avoid sharing confidential details until the firm confirms a secure way to provide them.
